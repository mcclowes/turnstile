# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short, be kind, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language aren't welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions, or anywhere else the project lives.

Report anything that breaks this to the maintainer privately, through the contact details at [mcclowes.com](https://mcclowes.com/). Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainer may remove content or ban people who don't follow it.
